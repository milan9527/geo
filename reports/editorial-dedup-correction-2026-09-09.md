# Editorial deduplication correction

Dated snapshot: **2026-09-09**. This English summary was rewritten from the historical report; later releases may supersede its state.

The first review incorrectly treated added background as sufficient originality for articles sharing the same core event. The correction examined all 325 full-text pairs among the 26 published articles using policy `2026-09-09-v2-core-coverage`.

All 26 met the quality threshold, but only fifteen were independently publishable; eleven were duplicates and were returned to review under the policy in effect on September 9. Manuscripts were retained. The retained category counts were Agent technology 4, commerce/media 3, AI 4, finance 2 and cloud 2.

Containment of an article's core contribution counted as duplication. Extra context, adjacent news or suggested tests did not automatically justify another publication. Shared companies, sources or templates alone were not decisive. Every duplicate was directly compared with its proposed retained version; uncertain decisions were not automatically approved.

The correction completed at 03:37:36 UTC, with 26 audit records in batch `editorial-b3fe14c3ab601e608f71`. Detailed reviews remain in `article_editorial_reviews` and private audit evidence.

**Historical policy:** the later [published-page protection](publication-protection-2026-09-10.md) prohibits withdrawing or deleting an existing public page during routine deduplication. This September 9 action is not the current workflow.
