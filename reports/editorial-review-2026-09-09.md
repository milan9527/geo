# Initial catalogue editorial review — superseded

Dated snapshot: **2026-09-09**. This English summary was rewritten from the historical report; later releases may supersede its state.

This first review was later corrected because it missed shared core content. Its classification is historical and must not be used as the current duplicate-free catalogue. See the [correction](editorial-dedup-correction-2026-09-09.md).

The initial snapshot contained 186 articles across published, review and draft states. Forty-five passed quality review; the first semantic pass classified 26 as independent, 90 as duplicates and 70 as needing changes or evidence. It computed full-text similarity for 17,205 pairs, screened all 186 catalogue entries and performed 156 candidate full-text reviews. No exact-body duplicates were found, illustrating why exact matching alone was insufficient.

That batch published 21 previously unpublished articles, retained five and returned ten former public articles to review. All manuscripts were retained. The subsequent full pairwise correction reduced the public catalogue to fifteen by identifying eleven additional core-content duplicates.

The batch ID was `editorial-c3c5c6513f85f02aa5f7`, with 186 records in `article_editorial_reviews`. Model decisions cannot guarantee mathematical uniqueness. The [September 10 protection policy](publication-protection-2026-09-10.md) later prohibited routine withdrawal or deletion of published pages.
